Notes for Reading This Menu

Prices are typical, not fixed. Most Tim Hortons restaurants are independently owned franchises, so a coffee can cost a little more at an airport, a campus or a highway stop than in your neighbourhood. Sales tax, from 5% GST to 15% HST, is added at the till.

Sizes matter. Brewed coffee comes in Small, Medium, Large and, at many restaurants, Extra Large. Specialty drinks usually stop at Large. Moving from Small to Medium on an Original Blend costs only 24 cents.

Boxes save money. A dozen donuts brings the price down to about $1.21 each, and a 50-pack of Timbits to about 20 cents each. Muffins, cookies and bagels also come in assorted boxes.

Check the app for exact prices. The Tim Hortons app shows prices for your restaurant, lets you order ahead and collects Tims Rewards points on eligible purchases.

Readers' Questions

How many items are on the Tim Horton menu?

This guide lists 130 popular items across nine categories, from coffee and Iced Capps to breakfast, lunch, donuts, Timbits, cookies, bakery items and bagels. Seasonal and regional items come and go during the year.

Does Tim Hortons have an Extra Large size?

Yes. Many restaurants offer Extra Large brewed coffee and tea, but most specialty and cold drinks come in Small, Medium and Large only.

What is Roll Up To Win?

Roll Up To Win is Tim Hortons' long-running spring contest. Eligible purchases give digital rolls in the Tims app that can win prizes such as free food and drinks.

What is Smile Cookie week?

Smile Cookie is Tim Hortons' charity campaign. For one week, proceeds from Smile Cookie sales go to local charities and community groups chosen by restaurant owners.

What is the best value item at Tim Hortons?

The 50-pack of Timbits is one of the best values at about 20 cents per Timbit, and a dozen donuts works out to about $1.21 each. For drinks, a Medium Original Blend at about $1.83 is hard to beat.

What should I order at Tim Hortons for the first time?

Try a Medium Double-Double with an Apple Fritter or a box of 10 Timbits. In summer, swap the coffee for an Original Iced Capp.

Is there a Tim Hortons lunch menu?

Yes. Lunch at Tim Hortons includes Loaded Wraps and Bowls in Habanero or Cilantro Lime, classic chicken wraps, a steak sandwich and flatbread pizza.

Is this the official Tim Hortons website?

No. This is an independent guide with typical Canadian prices. Use the Tim Hortons app or official website for exact prices at your restaurant.
